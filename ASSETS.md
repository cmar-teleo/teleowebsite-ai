## Fresco plates

All in `public/plates/`, all made by `scripts/paint-plates.mjs` (`npm run paint`).

**Language:** dark-ground fresco. Palette: vine black `#14110e`, chalk white `#e8e0cc`, yellow ochre `#c9993c`, red ochre `#a2452a`, earth green `#66724a`, Egyptian blue `#31609f` (sky wash only).

**Method:** the script composes each plate as SVG and rasterises it with sharp.
- Every form is built from individual brush strokes whose paths are jittered by a seeded random walk, with varying width and opacity, so fills are streaky and edges uneven.
- A pigment filter displaces the edges, then punches fine and broad noise through the paint so the plaster shows in specks and thin patches.
- The wall is a separate mottled plaster ground with pale worn patches and jagged cracks that have a chipped light edge.
- Underdrawing (sinopia) is red-ochre line only, doubled and searching, with a plumb line and measure ticks.
- No gradients, lighting, highlights, perspective projection or glow are used.

These are generated by code, not painted by hand. Each is an ordinary image and can be replaced by a commissioned painting (see README).

## Open Graph images

43 JPEGs at 1200 x 630 in `public/og/`. Method: `scripts/generate-og.mjs` takes the finished hero fresco as the ground, darkens the left for the page's own heading, adds one orange rule, and composites the real logo file on a white plate. Regenerate with `npm run og`.
